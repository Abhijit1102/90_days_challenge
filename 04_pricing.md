# Pricing Strategy Guidelines

- **Never quote only one price.** Provide multiple tiers or options to give prospects choices and anchor their expectations.
- **Build packages around value.** Focus package features on outcomes and client return-on-investment rather than just deliverables or hours.
- **Make the middle package your core offer.** Design the intermediate option as the primary target—providing the ideal balance of scope, value, and margin.
- **Never make the cheapest package useless.** Ensure the entry-level tier still delivers real results, maintaining trust and preserving a viable baseline standard.
